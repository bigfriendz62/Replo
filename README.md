# Replo — Your AI Business Partner

> The AI employee every small business owner can afford.

## Features (Version 1)
- ⚡ Smart Reply Engine — instant professional customer replies
- ✦ Daily Content Generator — posts for Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok
- ◈ Business Advisor Chat — ask anything about your business
- ▦ Message Templates Library — 200+ copy-paste WhatsApp templates
- ◎ Startup Guide — business ideas and 30-day launch plan (Pro)
- ◆ Marketing Planner — campaign plans for any event (Pro)
- ● Business Health Check — weekly performance review (Pro)
- ▤ Invoice Generator — professional invoices instantly (Pro)

## Pricing
- Free: 10 AI replies/day + templates + basic advisor
- Pro: $8/month or $55/year — unlimited everything
